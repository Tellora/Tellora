## 3. ON-PAGE OPTIMIZATION RULES
1. **Heading Hierarchy**: 
    - `H1` (Unique, contains primary keyword)
    - `H2` (Main sections, LSI keywords)
    - `H3` (Sub-sections)
2. **Keyword Density**: 1.5% - 2.5% for primary keyword.
3. **Internal Linking**: Minimum 3 internal links per blog post.
4. **External Linking**: Minimum 1-2 links to high-authority external resources.
5. **Image SEO**: Every image must have a descriptive `alt` tag and be `< 100KB`.

## 4. E-E-A-T SIGNALS
- **Experience**: Showcase case studies with real data.
- **Expertise**: Author profiles for blog posts.
- **Authoritativeness**: Link to external press or social proof.
- **Trust**: HTTPS, clear contact info, and transparent team profiles.

## 5. REVENUE-DRIVEN CONTENT
- Focus on "Bottom of Funnel" (BOFU) keywords: "Best SEO agency for D2C", "Hire performance marketing team".
- Case studies must lead with **ROI** and **Growth %**.
